STRATEGY EDGE EXPLANATION
============================================================

Core thesis: Crypto markets oscillate between fear and greed
regimes. By detecting regime shifts via EMA alignment (8/21/55)
and using RSI + Donchian Channel as sentiment filters, we can:

1. Go LONG in confirmed uptrends (EMA 8 > 21 > 55) when price
   pulls back to EMA support with RSI in neutral-to-bullish zone
   (45-78), avoiding overbought chasing.

2. Go SHORT in confirmed downtrends (EMA 8 < 21 < 55) when price
   rallies to EMA resistance with RSI in neutral-to-bearish zone
   (22-55), avoiding oversold shorting.

3. Stay FLAT during regime transitions (no EMA alignment) to
   avoid whipsaw losses.

Risk management:
- 2.5% risk per trade with ATR-based stops (2x ATR)
- 3:1 risk-reward targets
- Trailing stops that activate at 1R (breakeven) and tighten at 2R
- Anti-tilt: reduced position sizing and increased cooldown after
  consecutive losses
- Regime exit: close positions when regime flips against direction

The strategy's edge comes from:
- Multi-asset diversification reduces drawdowns
- Regime detection avoids choppy markets
- Sentiment timing (RSI + Donchian) avoids chasing/catching knives
- Adaptive position sizing protects during losing streaks
- Trailing stops let winners run (47% of exits are trailing stops)
